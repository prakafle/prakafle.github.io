Statistical analysis and machine learning on large-scale collision data at the Large Hadron Collider, in the 5,000+ member ATLAS collaboration.

Photon isolation calibration (Run 3)

  • Applied a new method in ATLAS to quantify uncertainties in the scale-factor measurement.

High-pT Higgs study
