Projects

Photon Isolation Calibration & Uncertainty Framework

ATLAS, CERN · 2022 – present

Problem: Simulation doesn't perfectly match real detector data, so the mismatch must be measured and corrected, with an honest error bar.

Approach: Derived a data-driven shift and scale factor, developed new methods to quantify the uncertainties, and built an end-to-end Python and C++ package that automates the derivation and propagates systematic uncertainties in one pipeline.

Result: Adopted in the official ATLAS Run 3 recommendation used by analyses across the collaboration.

Tools: C++, Python, ROOT, uncertainty quantification

Machine-Learning Signal Classification

ATLAS, CERN

Problem: Rare Higgs signal events are buried under far more numerous background events with similar features.

Approach: Engineered physics-motivated features and trained boosted-decision-tree and neural-network classifiers to discriminate signal from background; used them to optimize di-Higgs and high-transverse-momentum Higgs search sensitivity.

Result: Improved signal/background separation; presented at the CAMPFIRE Workshop (2023).

Tools: Python, scikit-learn, boosted decision trees, neural networks, feature engineering

Selection Optimization & Bias Control (H→γγ)

ATLAS, CERN

Problem: A tighter selection raises purity but loses efficiency, and it can distort (sculpt) the background distribution. This is the same problem as overfitting and look-ahead bias in backtesting.

Approach: Built a framework to scan a new selection cut, optimized it on efficiency vs. purity, explicitly measured the sculpting it induced, and computed the K-factor correction.

Result: A tuned, bias-checked selection for the differential cross-section measurement; presented at the HGamma meeting (2023).

Tools: Python, ROOT, optimization, bias control

Monte Carlo Simulation of Neutrino Propagation

Harvard University · 2021

Problem: Estimate how tau neutrinos propagate through the Earth to inform the design of the proposed TAMBO detector.

Approach: Wrote stochastic Monte Carlo simulations in Python and C++.

Result: Simulation inputs for the detector design study.

Tools: Python, C++, Monte Carlo

Numerical Solution of the 2D Schrödinger–Newton Equation

Reed College · 2021 – 2022

Problem: A nonlinear PDE coupling quantum mechanics and gravity, with no closed-form solution.

Approach: Implemented numerical solvers for the two-dimensional case.

Result: Undergraduate research; later presented as "Two-Body Gravity and Quantum Mechanics" (MSU, 2026).

Tools: numerical methods, PDEs
